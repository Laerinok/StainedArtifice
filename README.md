# 🏺 Stained Artifice
> *Elevate your builds with artisanal light and medieval prestige.*

**Stained Artifice** is a dedicated expansion for builders seeking to add a unique "artisanal" touch to their creations. Designed specifically to complement **medieval, gothic, and ecclesiastical** architectural styles, this mod brings new life to your windows through the ancient art of leaded glass.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎨 Complex Artistic Motifs
Discover a vast collection of leaded stained glass with intricate designs. This isn't just colored glass; it's art.
*   **Celestial Compass** 🧭
*   **Clock of the Arborescent Dawn** 🌳
*   **The Great Mechanism** ⚙️
*   **Void-Stained Eternity** 🌌
*   *And many more...*

### 🧱 Seamless Rock Integration
Unlike standard glass, these windows are embedded in frames that adapt perfectly to your architecture:
*   **12 Rock Types:** Full compatibility with Andesite, Basalt, Granite, and all other vanilla rocks.
*   **3 Finishes:** Frames automatically match your walls in **Ashlar**, **Cobblestone**, or **Polished Rock**.

### 📐 Versatile Shapes and Formats
Break the square grid with various window geometries:
*   **Standard:** Square and Round versions (1x1).
*   **Elegant:** Vertical Rectangles (1x2).
*   **Grandeur:** Large Composite Windows (2x2), perfect for cathedral rose windows or grand hall openings.

---

## 🛠️ New Crafting System
To craft these masterpieces, you will need to master new tools and materials:
*   **Glassmaker Tools:** The specialized kit required for delicate work.
*   **Lead Cames:** Authentic lead strips used to assemble the glass fragments.
*   **Pattern System:** Discover and use specific patterns for each unique motif.

---

## 📖 Getting Started
Check the **Survival Handbook (H)** in-game for the full *Quick Start Guide*! It covers everything from preparing your lead cames to selecting the right stone for your frames.

---

### 📥 Installation
1. Download the latest release.
2. Drop the `.zip` into your Vintage Story `mods` folder.
3. Restart the game and start crafting!
